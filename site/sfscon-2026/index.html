PA Webinar workshop at SFSCON 2026

In 40 minutes you use PA Webinar in three ways: as a participant in a live room, as the organiser of your own event, and as the person who installs it.

  • Friday 13 November 2026, 17:00–17:40
  • Crane Hall, Workshop B · NOI Techpark, Bolzano
  • Daniele Pizzolli in the room, Raffaele Vitiello connected online
PA Webinar is a beta

It already runs public events, and it is still changing. What you notice today goes into the next releases.

How today’s workshop is wired

Everything you use today runs on one Kubernetes cluster in the Azure Italy North region, and all the rooms share one video bridge.

What to bring

  • A laptop with a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox. A phone is enough for the shared room.
  • Headphones. Twenty laptops with open speakers in one room make echo and feedback. Keep your microphone off unless you are speaking.
  • Nothing to install. If you want PA Webinar on your own laptop, install it at home: the download is about 2.7 GB and the room network cannot carry it for everyone. See Install it yourself.

The 40 minutes

17:00

Welcome · 5 minutes

Open this page from the QR code on the screen. We go through the plan.

17:05

Join a live room · 10 minutes

Everybody joins the shared room as a guest. Raise your hand, write in the chat, ask a question in Q&A, answer a poll and add a word to the “In one word” cloud. Raffaele moderates and gives the floor to someone in the room.

17:15

Run your own event · 15 minutes

Groups of three or four, each with its own PA Webinar instance: create an event, register, start the room, moderate. Daniele notes where you get stuck. This is the first usability test of the organiser screens. The steps.

17:30

Install it yourself · 5 minutes

Raffaele shows an installation running on a laptop and the four ways to install. You do it later, with the commands in Install it yourself.

17:35

Feedback and questions · 5 minutes

Back in the shared room. When Raffaele closes it, a short questionnaire appears for everybody. Then your questions.

The shared room

Join the shared room

The room opens on Friday at 16:45. Before then the link shows a waiting room.

  1. Open the link, type your name and choose Enter now. You do not need to register.
  2. You enter with microphone and camera off. Leave the microphone off: Daniele’s laptop is the only open microphone in the room, and it plays Raffaele’s voice.
  3. The panels are on the right on a laptop and at the bottom on a phone: Chat, Q&A, Polls, In one word, Agenda, Materials, Participants.

Run your own event

Each group gets one PA Webinar instance, installed for the workshop: you are its administrator and can change anything. At 17:15 the instance key of each group is posted in the chat of the shared room. The instances are deleted on Friday evening, with everything in them.

The instances

Each instance has a mailbox: every email it sends ends up there, and nothing leaves the server. Use invented addresses, such as anna@example.org. Mailbox user: the instance name (ws1, ws2…), password: the instance key.

The exercise at a glance

The steps

Decide who does what: one organiser, who also moderates the room, and the others as participants. Swap roles if there is time.

  1. Organiser

    Open the portal of your instance, choose Sign in with the instance key and paste the key from the chat.

  2. Organiser

    Go to Events → New event and answer the four questions:

    • How many people do you expect? Up to 20
    • Who can speak and appear on video? Everyone
    • Do you want to be able to record? No: recording is not installed on these instances
    • Who can take part? Anyone who registers

    Then Continue.

  3. Organiser

    Fill in the six steps and choose Publish event at the end. Three things to know:

    • The title and the description in Italian are required, even with the screens in English. PA Webinar is made for the Italian public administration, so Italian is always the main language. Write whatever you like there; Change languages adds English.
    • In When, set today and a start time a few minutes from now.
    • In People, the lead moderator is you, with an invented address.
  4. Participants

    Open the public page of the event: the organiser shares it with Copy public link or shows its QR code. Choose Register for this event and use an invented address.

  5. Everybody

    Open the mailbox of your instance. Emails arrive within a minute: the registration confirmations for the participants, the moderator link for the organiser.

  6. Organiser

    Open the moderator link, then Join as moderator, Start event and Start now.

  7. Participants

    Open your link from the confirmation email, type your name and choose Enter now. Microphone and camera start off: turn them on only with headphones.

  8. Everybody

    Try what you would use in a real meeting: raise your hand, chat, Q&A, a poll, “In one word”, the agenda. The organiser answers raised hands with Give the floor and finds the room controls in the Control panel.

  9. Organiser

    Close with Leave room → End for everyone. Then open the event in the administration area and look at the People, Statistics and After the event tabs.

If something does not work

The key is refused

Copy it again from the chat, without spaces at the start or at the end. Each key works only on its own instance.

No email in the mailbox

The instance sends emails once a minute: wait a minute and reload the mailbox. Check that you opened the mailbox of your own instance.

“Enter now” does not appear, or the room is getting ready

The event has not started yet. The organiser opens the moderator link and chooses Start event, then Start now.

Nobody hears me, or there is echo

Use headphones and check that your microphone is on in the room toolbar. If two laptops in the same group have open speakers, mute one of them.

Still stuck

Raise your hand in the shared room, or call Daniele. Note what blocked you: it is exactly what we want to hear in the questionnaire.

Install it yourself

PA Webinar runs on Kubernetes, and Docker Compose is enough to look at the portal. All the commands below use the same version as the group instances: commit d2162afd of the main branch, with the images tagged dev-f4021b1.

Always pass the tag

Without --tag, the scripts on main use the dev image, which changes with every push to the development branch and may not match the code you checked out.

  1. Docker Compose

    For development

    Look at the portal and the code on one computer, with demo events.

    You need
    Docker with Compose
    Measured
    about 1.4 GB, a minute and a half

    Development guide

  2. minikube

    Tested in labEvaluation, not events

    The whole platform on a Linux laptop, rooms included. Browsers join only from that laptop.

    You need
    4 CPUs, 8 GB of memory, 15 GB of disk
    Measured
    2.65 GB, 3 minutes 33 seconds

    minikube guide

  3. k3s

    Tested in labNo real event yet

    Small production on your own servers, with one command. No high availability.

    You need
    one server with 4 vCPUs and 8 GiB for about 20 people on camera, or three VMs

    k3s guide

  4. In the cloud

    AKS: runs real eventsEKS, GKE: not verified yet

    Production, with video bridges that scale to zero between events, a TURN relay and object storage.

    You need
    a managed Kubernetes cluster with a node pool for the bridges

    AKS · EKS · GKE

From your laptopto production

Docker Compose, step by step

git clone https://github.com/italia/pa-webinar.git
cd pa-webinar
git checkout d2162afd
scripts/compose-up.sh --images registry --tag dev-f4021b1

At the end the script prints the addresses of the portal and of the test mailbox.

minikube, step by step

You need Linux, Docker, minikube 1.38.1 or later, Helm 3.16.3 or later, kubectl, openssl and curl. No credentials are needed.

git clone https://github.com/italia/pa-webinar.git
cd pa-webinar
git checkout d2162afd
scripts/minikube-up.sh --images registry --tag dev-f4021b1
  1. Open the conference address that the script prints (https://jitsi.<ip>.nip.io) once and accept its certificate, then do the same with the portal. Accepting only the portal is not enough: the room does not load.
  2. The instance key is in ~/.config/pa-webinar/minikube/pa-webinar/secrets.yaml, as ADMIN_API_KEY.
  3. Sign in at https://app.<ip>.nip.io/en/admin/login with Sign in with the instance key, then follow the same steps as in the workshop.
  4. The emails arrive in the mailbox at https://mail.<ip>.nip.io.

Recording and the post-production on GPU need images that are not public yet, so they stay off in this setup. macOS and Windows have not been tested.

Feedback

At 17:35 Raffaele closes the shared room, and the questionnaire appears on every screen at the same time. It takes a minute and does not ask for your name. It runs inside PA Webinar itself, so the answers stay on the same servers as the rooms. If you close the tab before the end, you miss it.

Later, for a bug or an idea, open an issue on github.com/italia/pa-webinar/issues.
